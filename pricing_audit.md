# AMES Food Advisory Pricing Audit

## Summary

The previous pricing page positioned staff training as a low-priced bolt-on at **$750 for up to 15 staff**. That price was too thin for a full-day, on-site, tailored manufacturing workshop and did not reflect the founder's TAFE NSW lecturing experience, manufacturing QA background, advance manual review, or the opportunity cost of taking a production team off the floor.

Training is now a standalone three-tier service category. The old training bolt-on has been removed.

## New training menu

| Tier | Price ex GST | Best for | Core scope |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Core Team Induction | $950 | A focused foundation session | 2.5–3 hours on-site, up to 15 participants, practical food safety fundamentals, attendance records and manager handover |
| Custom Compliance Workshop | $1,950 | Tailored food and work instruction | Full day on-site, up to 15 participants, advance review of the supplied manual, tailored food safety and site-specific work instructions, competency checks and manager debrief |
| Audit-Ready Masterclass | $2,750 | Manufacturers preparing for an audit, customer review or process change | Everything in Tier 2 plus pre-session records review, on-floor mini gap analysis, written priority action summary and management debrief |

The listed prices cover one delivery block for up to 15 participants. Additional shifts, repeat delivery, travel outside Greater Sydney, formal WHS risk assessments, accredited high-risk work training and substantial document rewriting are scoped separately in writing.

## Commercial rationale

The new architecture separates **training delivery** from **system development**. This prevents a low-cost training line from anchoring the entire business and makes it clear that tailored preparation, on-site delivery, competency checks and manufacturing readiness are premium professional services. The manufacturer offer is now explicitly tiered by scope: Core for a tightly defined one-site project, Plus for higher complexity, and Complex/Multi-site for broader programmes.

The recommended response to the Silverwater enquiry is Tier 2 at **$1,950 + GST**, with Tier 3 offered at **$2,750 + GST** if Ax wants the site gap analysis and written action summary. The website now supports that conversation directly.
