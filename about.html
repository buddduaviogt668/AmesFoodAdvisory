Food safety advice you can actually trust

AMES Food Advisory was founded by George Skarmoutsos, a food technologist with over 11 years of hands-on quality assurance experience at The Arnott's Group — one of Australia's most recognised food manufacturers. That means we've seen it all: from supplier audits and HACCP reviews to full-scale production QA and regulatory compliance.

We don't sell generic templates downloaded from the internet. Every HACCP plan, every SOP, every training session is built from scratch for your specific business. Because when an inspector walks through your door, a template won't save you — but a properly designed food safety program will.

Who we work with

  • Cafes & restaurants — Food safety programs, staff training, and audit prep across Sydney and NSW.
  • Food manufacturers — HACCP plans, QA system design, and GFSI readiness (SQF, BRC).
  • Food startups — From concept to compliant: council registration, kitchen design review, label validation.
  • RTOs & training providers — Curriculum development, assessment tools, and learner resources for food qualifications.
  • Aged care, childcare, and hospitality — Sector-specific compliance programs meeting NSW Food Authority standards.

What sets us apart

  • Real manufacturing experience — Not a consultant who read about HACCP in a textbook. We've lived it on the production floor.
  • TAE-qualified trainer — Your staff actually retain what they learn. We know how to teach adults effectively.
  • TAFE NSW lecturer — Currently teaching Food Science & Technology. Our methods are current and industry-aligned.
  • Fixed-price packages — No hourly billing surprises. You know the cost upfront.
  • No gatekeeping — We teach you how to maintain your systems. You're not locked into ongoing consulting fees to stay compliant.

Let's talk

Every engagement starts with a free 15-minute scoping call. We'll discuss your business, identify the gaps, and give you a clear picture of what's needed — no obligation, no sales pitch.
