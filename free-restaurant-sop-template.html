SOP 01 — Receiving & Inspecting Deliveries
Responsible:
Frequency: Every delivery
Records Required: Delivery log, temperature record
Review Date:
1.Check vehicle hygiene and driver compliance before unloading.
2.Inspect packaging for damage, leaks, or tampering. Reject damaged goods.
3.Probe-check temperature of chilled goods (≤5°C) and frozen goods (≤−15°C) using calibrated thermometer.
4.Check use-by / best-before dates. Reject anything past date or with insufficient shelf life.
5.Record delivery details: date, time, supplier, product, temperature, condition, accepted/rejected.
6.Store immediately in correct temperature zone — chilled goods within 15 minutes of receipt.
7.Apply FIFO (First In, First Out) — rotate stock so older items are used first.
SOP 02 — Temperature Monitoring & Recording
Responsible:
Frequency: Min. twice daily
Records Required: Temperature log
Review Date:
1.Check all refrigerators at opening and before service. Target: ≤5°C. Corrective action if >8°C.
2.Check hot holding equipment. Target: ≥60°C. Corrective action if <57°C for >30 min.
3.Record all readings on the temperature monitoring log with time and initials.
4.Calibrate probe thermometer weekly using ice slurry (0°C ±1) and boiling water (100°C ±1).
5.If equipment fails temperature targets, place out of service, transfer food, and notify manager immediately.
6.Retain temperature logs for minimum 3 months.
SOP 03 — Cooking & Reheating
Responsible:
Frequency: Every cooking/reheating event
Records Required: Cooking temperature log
Review Date:
1.Cook all poultry, minced meat, and stuffed meats to minimum internal temperature of 75°C (measured at thickest point).
2.Cook whole cuts of beef, pork, and lamb to minimum 63°C (with 3-minute rest) unless customer requests otherwise.
3.Reheat all previously cooked food to minimum 75°C throughout — never reheat more than once.
4.Probe at multiple points for thick items. Wipe and sanitise thermometer probe between uses.
5.Record: food item, target temp, actual temp, time, and initials.
6.Do not serve food that has not reached the required internal temperature — return to heat.
SOP 04 — Cooling Cooked Food
Responsible:
Frequency: Every cooling event
Records Required: Cooling log
Review Date:
1.Remove food from heat. Divide into shallow containers (max 10cm depth) to speed cooling.
2.Cool from 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours. Methods: blast chiller, ice bath, shallow pans.
3.Cool from 21°C to 5°C within a further 4 hours. Total cooling time must not exceed 6 hours.
4.Record start temperature, intermediate temperature (at 2 hrs), and final temperature with times.
5.Discard any food that has not cooled within the required timeframe.
6.Label cooled food with date and time. Store covered, labelled, and dated.
SOP 05 — Allergen Management
Responsible:
Frequency: Ongoing / per service
Records Required: Allergen matrix, training records
Review Date:
1.Maintain an up-to-date allergen matrix for all menu items (14 FSANZ priority allergens).
2.When a customer declares an allergy: confirm with chef, do not guess — check ingredient labels and specs.
3.Prepare allergen-free dishes on clean surfaces using dedicated utensils and equipment (no shared fryers).
4.Wash hands thoroughly before handling allergen-free meals.
5.Communicate allergen requests clearly through the pass — flag the plate/ticket visually.
6.If unsure whether a dish can be made allergen-safe, tell the customer — never guess.

SOP 07 — Personal Hygiene & Illness Policy
Responsible: All staff
Frequency: Ongoing
Records Required: Illness report log
Review Date:
1.Wash hands: on arrival, after toilet, after handling raw food, after handling waste, after touching face/hair.
2.Wear clean uniform each shift. No jewellery except plain wedding band. Hair tied back or covered.
3.Do not handle food if suffering from: diarrhoea, vomiting, jaundice, sore throat with fever, infected skin wounds.
4.Report illness to supervisor before commencing work. Complete illness report log.
5.Cover any cuts or wounds with a brightly coloured (blue) waterproof bandage and gloves.
6.No eating, drinking (except water in sealed container), or smoking in food preparation areas.
