About This Checklist

Use this self-assessment 2–4 weeks before a planned audit (council inspection, supplier audit, or third-party certification). Work through each section and mark your status honestly. Failed items become your remediation priority list.

Audit Type:    Date:

Day 1–3
Complete this checklist & identify all failed items
Day 4–7
Fix critical & high-priority non-conformances
Day 8–14
Update records, refresh training, run mock walk-through

Audit Day
Brief staff, have records accessible, stay calm
1. Documentation Readiness
Food Safety Program (FSP) current and accessible — dated, signed, version-controlled. HIGH
Temperature monitoring logs complete — at least 3 months of records on-site, no gaps.
Cleaning schedule sign-off sheets complete — daily records for last month with no missing dates.
Staff training records current — FSS certificate not expired, food handler training documented. HIGH
Delivery/receiving records complete — supplier, date, temperature, condition logged.
Pest control reports on file — at least 2 consecutive service reports available.
Corrective action log maintained — evidence that issues were found and acted upon.
Allergen matrix documented and linked to current menu.
Supplier list current — all active suppliers listed with contact details.
2. Physical Premises
Floors, walls, and ceilings in good repair — no cracks, peeling paint, or rust that could contaminate food. HIGH
Equipment clean and in good working order — no broken parts, damaged surfaces, or malfunctions.
No evidence of pest activity — no droppings, nesting, gnaw marks, or live pests observed. HIGH
Handwash basins fully stocked and accessible — soap, paper towels, and running hot water.
Lighting adequate — no broken lights, sufficient illumination at prep surfaces.
Ventilation working correctly — no condensation, adequate airflow, canopy functioning.
Chemical storage correct — chemicals stored separately, labelled, locked or segregated.
Waste management adequate — bins lidded, emptied regularly, storage area clean.
3. Food Handling Practices
Staff observed following correct handwashing procedure — frequency and method correct. HIGH
No cross-contamination risks observed — raw and ready-to-eat foods separated, correct boards used.
Temperature control practices correct — food not left in danger zone (5°C–60°C) without monitoring.
Correct cooling procedures followed — no large volumes of hot food refrigerated in bulk without blast chilling.
Labelling correct — all stored food labelled with product name and date.
FIFO applied correctly — oldest stock at front, no out-of-date products in storage.
4. Staff Knowledge
Staff can state critical temperatures — storage, cooking, and hot holding temperatures known. MED
Staff know what to do if a temperature deviation occurs — corrective action steps understood.
Staff can identify major allergens and explain the business allergen procedure.
Staff know illness reporting policy — when not to work and who to contact.
FSS certificate holder is on-site and identifiable during audit hours. HIGH
5. Compliance & Licencing
Food business notification current with local council. HIGH
NSW Food Authority licence current (if in a licensed sector) — not expired.
Previous audit non-conformances closed — evidence of corrective actions on file.
No outstanding council orders or improvement notices.
6. Post-Audit Action Plan
All critical non-conformances have an owner and due date for remediation.
Photographic evidence taken of issues and fixes for the corrective action log.
Audit findings shared with all relevant staff in a briefing.
Next internal review date scheduled — within 3 months of this audit.
