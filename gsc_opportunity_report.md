# GSC Opportunity Report — AMES Food Advisory

**Source:** `amesfoodadvisory.com.au-Performance-on-Search-2026-08-20.xlsx` supplied by the user. This report uses the exported Queries, Pages, Countries and Devices tabs and focuses on actionable SEO and conversion opportunities.

## Executive conclusion

AMES is already earning meaningful visibility for high-intent NSW regulatory topics, but the biggest growth constraint is not lack of relevance. It is **low click-through rate at positions where the site is already visible**, especially for “NSW Food Authority”, “HACCP meaning”, “food safety program”, and “food safety inspections”. The fastest path is to improve search-result packaging and turn those informational pages into explicit premium-service entry points.

Track these changes in the next GSC comparison: CTR for the registration, HACCP and Food Safety Program pages; clicks from “NSW Food Authority”, “HACCP meaning” and “food safety program”; clicks to pricing and Calendly; and enquiry quality by service type.
