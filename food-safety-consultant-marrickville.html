Conversational Q&A

Questions & Answers

How often does Inner West Council inspect food businesses?
Inner West Council typically inspects food businesses one to three times per year depending on their risk classification. High-risk businesses (those handling unpackaged ready-to-eat food) are inspected most frequently. We ensure you are always inspection-ready.
Can you help a Marrickville brewery comply with Standard 3.2.2A?
Absolutely. Standard 3.2.2A is the federal standard that came into force in December 2023 requiring food businesses to have a Food Safety Supervisor and trained staff. Most Marrickville taprooms and food-serving venues fall under this requirement. We handle the full implementation.

Local Knowledge

We Know Marrickville

Marrickville is home to Marrickville Metro, the Addison Road Community Centre market, Peters Ice Cream heritage factory site, Sydenham Road industrial precinct, and the inner west's craft brewery cluster. The area's craft brewing, small-batch food manufacturing, cafés, bakeries, distilleries sector operates under Inner West Council regulations — and we know exactly what local inspectors look for.

Marrickville has become Sydney's craft food and beverage capital — Young Henrys, Grifter Brewing, Wildflower, Batch Brewing, and dozens of food producers are based here. AMES Food Advisory works on-site with local businesses to make sure your food safety documentation, HACCP plans, and staff training are ready before any council visit.
