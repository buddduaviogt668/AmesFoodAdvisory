Case Study: Home-Based Food Business

Compliance & Regulatory Setup for Home-Based Food Production

Chew Boy
The Challenge
A passionate home-based cookie business needed to formalize their operations, requiring official NSW Food Authority registration and a setup that complied with strict regulatory standards for both direct sales and cafe distribution.
The AMES Solution
Conducted a comprehensive on-site visit to audit the home production environment. Guided the client through the NSW Food Authority registration process and implemented a professional compliance framework.
The Outcome
Successfully registered and compliant food business. Chew Boy now operates with professional-grade batch traceability and a documentation suite that meets all NSW food safety requirements for wholesale distribution.
Key Deliverables Provided
  • FPAR (Food Program Audit Report)
  • FPAR Appendix (Site-Specific Documentation)
  • Ingredient Batch Traceability Templates
  • Regulatory Compliance Framework
