Understanding Sutherland Shire Council Food Inspections

Every food business operating within the jurisdiction of Sutherland Shire Council is subject to routine health inspections by local Environmental Health Officers (EHOs). These unannounced visits are designed to ensure your premises complies strictly with the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code.

Failing an inspection in the Sutherland Shire Council area can result in costly fines, mandatory improvement notices, or in severe cases, the temporary closure of your business and placement on the "Name and Shame" register.

How AMES Food Advisory Helps You Pass

We take the stress out of unannounced council audits. Our principal consultant brings over 11 years of Tier-1 manufacturing experience at The Arnott's Group, combined with deep pedagogical expertise as a TAFE NSW Lecturer.

We apply this corporate rigor to your small or medium-sized business through:

  • Mock EHO Inspections: We assess your premises using the exact criteria Sutherland Shire NSW Food Authority auditors use, identifying critical non-conformances before the real audit occurs.
  • Customised SOPs: We write and implement practical standard operating procedures (cleaning, temperature control, allergen management) tailored to your kitchen.
  • Staff Coaching: Unlike generic click-through courses, our hands-on coaching is designed by a TAE-Qualified trainer to ensure your staff actually retain and practice food safety daily.
