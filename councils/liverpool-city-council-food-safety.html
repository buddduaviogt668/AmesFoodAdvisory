Important Clarification: AMES Food Advisory does not conduct official council inspections, nor are we an RTO or a regulatory body. We are a specialized consulting firm that prepares your business to successfully pass official Environmental Health Officer (EHO) inspections conducted by Liverpool City Council and the NSW Food Authority.

Understanding Liverpool City Council Food Inspections

Every food business operating within the jurisdiction of Liverpool City Council is subject to routine health inspections by local Environmental Health Officers (EHOs). These unannounced visits are designed to ensure your premises complies strictly with the Australia New Zealand Food Standards Code.

Failing an inspection in the Liverpool City Council area can result in costly fines, mandatory improvement notices, or in severe cases, the temporary closure of your business and placement on the "Name and Shame" register.

How AMES Food Advisory Helps You Pass

We take the stress out of unannounced council audits. Our principal consultant brings over 11 years of Tier-1 national food manufacturing experience, combined with deep pedagogical expertise as a TAFE NSW Lecturer.

We apply this corporate rigor to your small or medium-sized business through:

  • Mock EHO Inspections: We assess your premises using the exact criteria Liverpool City Council inspectors use, identifying critical non-conformances before the real audit occurs.
  • Customised SOPs: We write and implement practical standard operating procedures (cleaning, temperature control, allergen management) tailored to your kitchen.
  • Staff Coaching: Unlike generic click-through courses, our hands-on coaching is designed by a TAE-Qualified trainer to ensure your staff actually retain and practice food safety daily.
