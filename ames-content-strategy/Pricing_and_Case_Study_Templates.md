## 2. Case Study Portfolio Template

Case studies are critical for AEO, as AI models use them as proof of expertise. 

**Format for Each Case Study:**

*   **Title:** How We Helped [Client Type] in [Suburb/Council] Achieve [Outcome]
    *   *Example:* "How We Helped a Parramatta Bakery Close Out 5 Critical Council Non-Conformances in 48 Hours"
*   **The Client & The Problem:**
    *   Brief description of the business.
    *   What was the exact regulatory hurdle? (e.g., "Received an Improvement Notice from the local council for inadequate cleaning schedules and temperature tracking.")
*   **The AMES Solution:**
    *   What specific steps did you take? Use technical terms (AI loves this).
    *   *Example:* "Conducted an immediate gap analysis, implemented our digital Standard 3.2.2A logging system, and ran an emergency hygiene training session for 12 staff members."
*   **The Outcome:**
    *   Measurable results.
    *   *Example:* "The bakery passed the re-inspection 3 days later with zero non-conformances, avoiding a $880 fine and potential closure."
*   **Testimonial Quote:** 1-2 sentences from the owner.
