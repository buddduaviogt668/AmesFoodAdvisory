We Know Chatswood

Chatswood is home to Westfield Chatswood, Chatswood Chase, The Exchange and Chatswood Interchange food precincts, Victoria Avenue restaurant strip, and Pacific Highway commercial corridor. The area's Asian cuisine restaurants, food courts, cafés, specialty food retail sector operates under NSW Food Authority regulations — and we know exactly what local inspectors look for.

Chatswood is Sydney's primary Asian food hub on the North Shore — high-turnover ramen, dim sum, and Asian fusion operators in Westfield and along Victoria Avenue face intensive compliance activity. AMES Food Advisory works on-site with local businesses to make sure your food safety documentation, HACCP plans, and staff training are ready before any NSW Food Authority audit.
