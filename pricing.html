Pricing questions

Common questions

If your question isn't here, the free scoping call is the fastest way to get an answer.

Why fixed prices instead of hourly rates? +
Hourly billing creates uncertainty. You don't know what you'll pay until the invoice arrives, and it creates the wrong incentive — the consultant benefits from taking longer. Fixed pricing means you know your full cost before work begins, and I have every reason to work efficiently.
What happens if my scope changes mid-project? +
If the scope changes materially from what was agreed, we discuss it before I proceed. Any variation is quoted in writing first. You're never hit with a surprise invoice for work you didn't authorise.
Is the $450 site audit really credited back in full? +
Yes — in full, with no conditions attached. If you proceed with any package after the site audit, $450 is deducted from your invoice. The audit is designed to give you independent, professional advice about what you need. You're never obligated to proceed.
Do prices include GST? +
All prices shown are exclusive of GST. GST will be itemised on your invoice where applicable.
How long does a typical engagement take? +
The Essentials package typically takes 1–2 weeks from site visit to delivery. Start-Up Package 2–4 weeks. The Manufacturer Compliance package 4–6 weeks depending on complexity. Timeframes are confirmed in writing before commencement.
Do you travel outside Greater Sydney? +
Yes — I work across all of NSW. Travel outside Greater Sydney is discussed upfront and any supplement is quoted before the engagement begins. Nothing is added after the fact.
Can I start with just the site audit and decide later? +
Absolutely. The site audit and gap report is a complete standalone service. Many clients use it to understand their compliance position before deciding whether — and how — to proceed. You're never pressured to commit to a package on the day.
