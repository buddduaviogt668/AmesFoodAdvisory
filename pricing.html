Pricing questions

Common questions

If your question isn't here, the free scoping call is the fastest way to get an answer.

Why fixed prices instead of hourly rates? +
Hourly billing creates uncertainty. You don't know what you'll pay until the invoice arrives, and it creates the wrong incentive — the consultant benefits from taking longer. Fixed pricing means you know your full cost before work begins, and I have every reason to work efficiently.
What happens if my scope changes mid-project? +
If the scope changes materially from what was agreed, we discuss it before I proceed. Any variation is quoted in writing first. You're never hit with a surprise invoice for work you didn't authorise. The Manufacturer Core package includes one consolidated client review round; further review rounds, additional sites, product families, SOPs, visits, or major process changes are quoted separately before work proceeds.
How is the Manufacturer Compliance package paid? +
The standard Manufacturer Core package is invoiced in three milestones: 50% at booking and commencement, 30% when the draft HACCP and SOP suite is submitted for review, and 20% at final delivery and training. Exact payment terms are confirmed in the service agreement.
Is the $595 site audit fee refundable? +
No — the site audit fee is non-refundable. It covers the on-site visit, professional assessment, and written gap report delivered within 48 hours. Payment is processed upfront via Stripe at the time of booking. Card payments are subject to Stripe's 1.7% processing fee.
Do prices include GST? +
All prices shown are exclusive of GST. GST will be itemised on your invoice where applicable.
How long does a typical engagement take? +
The Essentials package typically takes 1–2 weeks from site visit to delivery. Start-Up Package 2–4 weeks. The Manufacturer Core package typically takes 4–6 weeks depending on how quickly information and consolidated feedback are provided. Complex, multi-site, or multi-product engagements are quoted separately with their own timeframe.
What if my operation is more complex than Manufacturer Core? +
Manufacturer Core is designed for one site, one defined process or product family, and up to 10 SOPs. Manufacturer Plus and complex or multi-site work are available from $6,500 and from $9,000 respectively, depending on product range, process complexity, number of sites, retailer requirements, visits, and remediation required.
Do you travel outside Greater Sydney? +
Yes — I work across all of NSW. Travel outside Greater Sydney is discussed upfront and any supplement is quoted before the engagement begins. Nothing is added after the fact.
Can I start with just the site audit and decide later? +
Absolutely. The site audit and gap report is a complete standalone service. Many clients use it to understand their compliance position before deciding whether — and how — to proceed. You're never pressured to commit to a package on the day.
