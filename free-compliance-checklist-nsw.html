NSW Food Business Compliance Checklist

47 essential checkpoints covering the NSW Food Act 2003, FSANZ Food Standards Code, and NSW Food Authority audit readiness. Tick off each item — then fix the gaps.

Regulatory BasisNSW Food Act 2003 · FSANZ Food Standards Code · NSW Food Authority Requirements · NSW Food Authority Guidelines
1. Business Registration & Licencing
Food business notification submitted to your NSW Food Authority under the NSW Food Act 2003. HIGH
Food Authority licence obtained (if operating in a licensed sector — meat, seafood, dairy, egg, plant products, or vulnerable persons). HIGH
Business ABN registered and listed on all food business documents.
Trade waste agreement in place with NSW Food Authority or water authority.
Development Application (DA) approved for food premises fitout if applicable.
2. Food Safety Supervisor & Staff Training
Food Safety Supervisor (FSS) appointed and certificate is current (renewed every 5 years). HIGH
FSS certificate displayed or available for inspection at the premises.
All food handlers trained in food safety fundamentals (food hygiene, temperature control, cross-contamination). MED
Training records maintained for all staff including induction dates and course certificates.
Illness and injury reporting procedure communicated to all food handlers.
3. Food Safety Program (FSP / HACCP)
Written Food Safety Program in place (mandatory for Class 1 & 2 businesses). HIGH
Hazard analysis completed — biological, chemical, and physical hazards identified for each process step.
Critical Control Points (CCPs) identified with documented critical limits (e.g. cook to 75°C core temp).
Monitoring procedures documented — who checks, how often, what records are kept. MED
Corrective action procedures documented for each CCP deviation.
FSP reviewed annually or when process/menu changes occur.
FSP accessible on-site and available for inspection.
4. Temperature Control
Cold storage maintained at 5°C or below. Fridge temperatures checked and recorded daily. HIGH
Hot holding maintained at 60°C or above. Temperature checked and recorded every 2 hours.
Calibrated probe thermometer available and used for cooking and cooling verification.
Cooling procedure documented — food cooled from 60°C to 21°C within 2 hours, then to 5°C within next 4 hours. HIGH
Temperature logs retained for minimum 3 months and available on request.
Freezer temperature maintained at −15°C or below.
5. Cleaning & Sanitation
Cleaning schedule documented — daily, weekly, and periodic tasks listed with responsible persons. MED
Food-safe sanitisers used at correct dilution. Dilution rates posted at handwash and cleaning stations.
Cleaning records signed off and retained for minimum 3 months.
Separate colour-coded chopping boards for different food types (raw meat, produce, ready-to-eat).
Chemical storage separated from food storage, preparation, and service areas.
Handwashing facilities adequate — dedicated hand basin with hot/cold water, soap, and paper towels at each prep area.
6. Allergen Management
14 FSANZ priority allergens identified in all menu items and documented. HIGH
Allergen information communicated to customers accurately — menus, verbal, or written on request.
Allergen cross-contact procedures documented and staff trained on them.
Supplier ingredient specifications checked for each ingredient to confirm allergen content.
7. Pest Control
Pest control contract in place with a licensed pest controller. MED
Pest control service reports retained and available on-site.
Entry points sealed — gaps around pipes, doors, and windows checked and sealed.
No evidence of pest activity — staff trained to report signs immediately.
8. Waste Management
Waste bins lidded and pest-proof, cleaned regularly.
Waste removed from food prep areas frequently — at least daily.
Grease trap maintained and serviced as per NSW Food Authority requirements.
9. Supplier & Traceability
Approved supplier list maintained — only licensed and reputable suppliers used. MED
Delivery records retained — date, supplier, product, temperature on receipt.
Batch/lot numbers recorded for high-risk ingredients to support recalls if needed.
Recall/withdrawal procedure documented and staff aware of it.
10. Premises & Equipment
Floors, walls, and ceilings smooth and cleanable in food preparation areas.
Adequate lighting in all food areas (minimum 240 lux at food prep surfaces).
Ventilation adequate — no condensation dripping onto food or surfaces.
Equipment maintained and in good repair — no rust, cracks, or damage that could contaminate food.
First aid kit available on premises.
